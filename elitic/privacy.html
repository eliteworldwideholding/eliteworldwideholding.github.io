Legal Framework

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

We prioritize user privacy and data minimization. To provide our language learning services, we only collect essential authentication data via Google OAuth (such as your basic profile info, email address, and unique user ID). This information is strictly used to identify your account and manage your AI token consumption quotas.

2. Processing of Text, URLs & Content

Our platform allows you to process custom text, web URLs, and news articles for language learning purposes. All content submitted for processing is sent directly to AI models in real time to generate translations, explanations, or exercises. We do not store, archive, or retain any of your submitted texts, URLs, or generated learning content on our servers.

3. AI Tokens & Data Usage

Your account data (user ID and email) is linked to a token counter to track your AI usage limits. The prompts sent to external AI service providers are ephemeral and used exclusively to deliver the immediate response requested by the application.

4. Third-Party Services (Google Auth)

We rely on Google Authentication for secure login. By using our application, you also interact with Google’s authentication ecosystem. We do not store passwords or secondary payment credentials on our infrastructure.

5. Data Retention & Security

Because we do not persist user-generated texts, articles, or browsing history on our servers, your privacy risk regarding data leaks or breaches is minimized by design. The minimal account identification data stored is encrypted and protected using industry-standard security measures.

6. User Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to request the complete deletion of your account identity and token usage history from our database at any time by contacting our support team or disconnecting your Google account permissions.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or how your authentication data is handled, feel free to reach out through our official platform channels.
